About the Project

The "Historical Epochs" Photoshow is your personal time machine. This project allows you to immerse yourself in the atmosphere of different historical periods by transforming your modern photograph into a portrait that looks like it's from another time. Whether you dream of being a noble in the Renaissance, a pharaoh of ancient Egypt, a knight of the medieval ages, or a flapper in the Roaring Twenties, I can create a series of images that bring that fantasy to life.
