🔹 Who We Are

Welcome to AI Gen Studio, a modern creative studio specializing in generative design. As an experienced ComfyUI developer and visual AI specialist, our focus is on professional image and video generation*. We are a design studio that uses the latest AI technology to create personalized media content. Our expertise spans the full AI video production pipeline—from advanced prompt engineering to final post-production.

💼 Our Expertise: Key Skills & Technologies

As a leading generative design studio, our work is powered by a deep understanding of core AI technologies. We are experts in creating compelling visual narratives through AI content creation.

  • AI & Diffusion Models: ComfyUI, Stable Diffusion 1.5 / SDXL / 3.5, WAN 2.1, ChilloutMix, MeinaMix, Taiyi
  • Video Generation: AnimateDiff, SVD-XT, Mochi, CogVideoX, MimicMotion, Face Fusion
  • Model Training: LoRA / LoCon / LyCORIS using kohya_ss, PEFT, Diffusers, Google Colab
  • Prompt Engineering: BLIP, CLIP Interrogator, LM Studio, ImageBind, Ollama
  • Node Development: Custom ComfyUI nodes, motion tracking, batch-video pipelines
  • Post-processing: DaVinci Resolve, Fusion, ffmpeg, Alpha rendering, retiming
  • Web Tools: FastAPI, Gradio, Flask, Vue.js, TailwindCSS
  • Other Tools: CUDA, OpenCV, Python, Auto1111, Runway, HeyGen, FaceSwap

🖼️ AI Image Generation Services

Our AI company excels at crafting unique visuals. This includes graph construction in ComfyUI for text2img, img2img, inpainting, style transfer, and advanced face-based generation using LoRA training and IPAdapter for perfect results.

  • Graph construction in ComfyUI: text2img, img2img, inpainting, style transfer
  • Face-based generation: LoRA training, IPAdapter (face insert), ControlNet + OpenPose
  • LoRA blending, masking, detail enhancement, VAE optimization

🎬 AI Video Production Services

We combine AI and creativity to produce dynamic videos. We offer everything from actor face generation via LoRA and AnimateDiff to complex video-to-video style transfers with consistent character appearance.

  • Actor/client face generation via LoRA + AnimateDiff
  • IPAdapter + AnimateDiff or CogVideoX from image
  • Face Fusion by keyframes or morphing series
  • Video-to-video with consistent face style
  • Post-processing: stabilization, alpha render, edit in Resolve

🧠 Custom Model Training (LoRA)

We provide specialized model training services to capture a specific face, character, or style from as few as 5-20 images, utilizing tools like kohya_ss and auto-captioning with BLIP.

  • Face/character training on 5–20 images
  • Auto-captioning: BLIP, DeepBooru
  • Cropping, padding, face enhancement
  • Tools: kohya_ss, PEFT, Colab, Diffusers

✏️ Advanced Prompt Engineering

At the core of great AI art is prompt engineering. We develop actor-specific prompts to control style, emotion, and pose, including sequential prompts for creating seamless morphing effects.

  • Actor-specific prompts: style, emotion, pose
  • Sequential prompts for morphing
  • Prompt generation via BLIP, CLIP, LM Studio, etc.

🧱 Custom Node & Pipeline Development

Our AI development services include creating custom ComfyUI nodes for LoRA creation, Face Extract/Merge, morph transitions, and integrating external APIs like Runway and HeyGen for a complete workflow.

  • Nodes for:
    • LoRA creation from photos
    • Face Extract + Merge
    • Morph transitions
    • External API integrations (Runway, HeyGen, LM API)
  • Support for FaceSwap, motion tracking, batch generation

🌐 Web Interface & Application Design

We build intuitive web interfaces for our AI solutions, allowing clients to manage the entire process from photo upload to final video export with options for LoRA/IPAdapter selection.

  • Custom UI for photo/video → generation → preview → export
  • Options for LoRA/IPAdapter selection and prompt generation
  • Built with FastAPI, Gradio, Vue, Tailwind

🎨 Professional Post-production

All our AI-generated content undergoes professional post-production in DaVinci Resolve, including color correction, background replacement, and final montage to ensure a high-quality result.

  • Portrait color correction, LUTs, skin tone balance
  • AI character/face insertion
  • Background replacement using AI + Chromakey
  • Final montage of avatars and morphing videos

📂 Sample Projects & Use Cases

Our portfolio showcases our ability to deliver complex projects, from client-face video generation to fully automated web pipelines that handle everything from photo upload to video export.

  • Client-face video generation from photo/video with emotion & style
  • 5-photo → LoRA → image series → video generation
  • Web pipeline: upload photo → generate video → preview & export
